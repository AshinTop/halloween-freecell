Classic manor

Move all 52 cards into the four foundations, from Ace to King in the same suit. Build the eight columns downward, alternating red and black. Any card can start an empty column. Each free cell holds one card.

Drag, or select and place

Drag a card or an ordered run to a glowing column, free cell or foundation. You can also tap the card, then tap its destination. Double-click or double-tap a single exposed card to try its foundation. A run needs enough free cells and empty columns. In short tables, use the scroll arrows; dragging near the top or bottom of the columns also scrolls them.

Lantern rescue

Three handcrafted endgames teach the art of making space. The same FreeCell rules apply. Each puzzle has a verified solution; the suggested move target is a friendly challenge, not a claim of the fewest possible moves.

Finish automatically

Once every remaining card can legally reach its foundation without another tableau move, the manor collects them automatically. Stop pauses collection; Undo restores the position before the collection began. After Stop or Undo, use Finish automatically to resume.

Hints suggest a legal move, not a guaranteed winning solution. Foundation cards may move back. Undo is unlimited. Shortcuts: U to undo, H for a hint, Esc to stop collection, cancel a drag, or clear your selection.
